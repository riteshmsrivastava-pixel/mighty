What's a Mighty Assist?

One Assist is one exchange with one person. Mighty writes the message, and rewrites it up to ten times. That is one Assist, not eleven.

Reading is free: the brief on someone, asking Mighty anything, searching the people you already know. Charging for a brief would be charging you to say no.

When they reply and you write back, that is a new Assist. Writing is the part worth paying for.

Uses an assist
  • Understanding a LinkedIn profile
  • Drafting a personalized outreach message
  • Summarizing coffee-chat notes
  • Preparing you for an upcoming meeting
  • Suggesting who to reconnect with
  • Recommending your next best networking move

Questions

Why is there no free plan?

Thirty days is enough to find out whether this changes how you work. A permanent free tier sounds generous and usually is not: it tunes the product to convert you rather than help you. If Mighty is not worth $9 after a month, it was not working.

What happens when my Assists run out?

Everything you have stays, and everything free stays free. What stops is Mighty writing for you. Buy a top-up, move up a plan, or wait for the month to turn.

Do top-ups expire?

No. Purchased Assists never expire and they stack on top of your monthly allowance. You bought them.

What happens after the thirty days?

Nothing is deleted. You can still read everything, ask questions, and export or delete the lot. A plan turns writing back on.

Can I pause?

Yes. Networking is seasonal. Pause the month, keep your data, stop the meter.

Why cap the number of relationships?

Because a list of five hundred people you do not remember adding is what Mighty exists to prevent. Close someone out before you add someone new.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your export is one click either way.
