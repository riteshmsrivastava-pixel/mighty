What's a Mighty Assist?

A Mighty Assist is when Mighty thinks with you. Only reasoning uses an assist. Viewing contacts, writing notes, organizing people, and searching your network never do.

Uses an assist
  • Understanding a LinkedIn profile
  • Drafting a personalized outreach message
  • Summarizing coffee-chat notes
  • Preparing you for an upcoming meeting
  • Suggesting who to reconnect with
  • Recommending your next best networking move

Questions

Why limit Mighty Assists?

AI is computationally expensive. A generous assist allowance in every plan keeps pricing affordable while ensuring everyone gets a fast, reliable experience.

What happens if I run out?

You keep full access to your contacts, notes, timelines, and organization tools. Just wait for your monthly assists to reset, or upgrade to Builder for more.

Can I buy extra assists?

Not today. We're keeping pricing simple.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term commitments.
