A few things you might be wondering.

Does saving someone send them a request?＋

No. Save to Mighty keeps the person and captured profile context for you. You decide whether and when to send a LinkedIn invitation or message.

How does Mighty decide who could be relevant?＋

Mighty compares available profile evidence with your saved goals and explains the connection. An investor could be relevant to fundraising; a senior leader might offer a career conversation. A fit label describes relevance, not a promise of funding, a job or a reply.

What stays with a saved person?＋

Their name, profile link, available photo and captured profile details. You can return to that snapshot, add your own notes, and keep your relationship history and next steps together. The snapshot depends on the information available when it was captured.

Will Mighty read my inbox and follow up for me?＋

You record what happened, including whether someone replied. Pulse brings recorded next actions into view. The current draft workflow lets you edit, copy and open LinkedIn; you decide when to send. Direct inbox connections are planned.

What is the difference between Ask and Ask Mighty?＋

Ask is the people-discovery view: find someone in your network or explore someone new. Ask Mighty is the conversational assistant for questions about your saved relationships and imported network.

Can I work toward more than one goal?＋

Yes. Keep a main goal and track another alongside it. Someone can be relevant to funding a venture even when they do not offer a clear route to your next job.

What can Rooms show me?＋

Rooms brings together people mentioned on an event page, so you can review their relevance and find their profiles. Being named on a page does not confirm attendance. Nearby suggestions and photo input are still planned.
