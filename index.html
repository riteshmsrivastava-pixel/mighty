Why MIghTy

LinkedIn is great at helping you find people.

It's not built to help you build relationships.

After a few weeks you start asking yourself:

?Have I already messaged this person?
?What did we talk about?
?Did they promise an introduction?
?Should I follow up now or wait?
?Who should I reach out to today?

MIghTy quietly keeps track of everything so you can focus on people, not on remembering details.

What it helps you do

01

Find the right people

Shortlist interesting people while browsing LinkedIn and organize them around your own goals.

02

Reach out thoughtfully

Generate personalized first drafts based on the person's actual background, not generic templates.

03

Remember every interaction

Capture coffee chats, introductions, notes and follow-ups in one relationship timeline.

04

Know what deserves attention

Every day MIghTy tells you which conversations are worth continuing and which relationships need attention.

Built for people who build relationships

Recruiting is one use case, not the product. Whatever you're building toward, MIghTy helps you stay intentional.

You stay in control
×MIghTy never sends messages for you.

Privacy

Your relationships belong to you.

Your messages, notes and relationship history remain private. Community insights are always anonymous and only shown after enough members have opted in.
