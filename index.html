Why Mighty

LinkedIn is great at helping you find people.

It was never built to help you know them.

Mighty quietly keeps track of everything, so you can spend your attention on people instead of on remembering details.

After a few weeks, the questions start:

Have I already messaged this person?
What did we actually talk about?
Did they promise an introduction?
Should I follow up now, or wait?
Who should I reach out to today?

What Mighty helps
you do

01

Find the right people

Shortlist people while you browse, and organise them around your own goals — not someone else's funnel.

02

Reach out thoughtfully

Drafts built from a person's actual background, in your voice. Nothing that reads like a template.

03

Remember every interaction

Coffee chats, introductions, notes and follow-ups gathered into one gentle timeline per person.

04

Know what deserves attention

Each day, a small nudge: these conversations are worth continuing, these relationships have gone quiet.

Every opportunity starts with someone.

Job hunting is one use case, not the product. Whoever you're building toward, Mighty helps you stay intentional about the people who get you there.

Privacy

Your relationships belong to you.

Your messages, notes and history stay private. Community insights are always anonymous, and only appear once enough members have opted in.
