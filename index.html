Why MIghTy

LinkedIn helps you find people. It doesn't help you build relationships.

After a few weeks, the questions pile up, and the details slip.

Have I already messaged this person?
What did we actually talk about?
Did they promise an introduction?
Should I follow up now, or wait?
Who should I reach out to today?

MIghTy keeps track of all of it, so you can focus on people, not on remembering details.

You stay in control

Human-in-the-loop by design

MIghTy never sends messages for you.
It never clicks Connect for you.
It never automates LinkedIn.

It helps you make better networking decisions while you stay in complete control. Every message is yours. Every send is your click.

Privacy

Your relationships belong to you.

Your messages, notes and relationship history stay private. Community insights are always anonymous, and only shown once enough members have opted in.
