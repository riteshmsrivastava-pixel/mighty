Install the Mighty extension

See goal relevance on LinkedIn, right on the profile, and save people to Mighty as you browse.

1

Download Mighty

Download the extension as a .zip.

Download Mighty extension
2

Unzip it

Double-click the file to unzip it. You get a folder called mighty-extension.

3

Open chrome://extensions

In Chrome, type chrome://extensions into the address bar and press Enter.

4

Turn on Developer mode

Turn on "Developer mode", top right.

5

Click "Load unpacked"

Click it, then choose the mighty-extension folder you unzipped in step 2.

6

Sign in

Click the Mighty icon in your toolbar and sign in. Same account as the web app.

You are in control

Mighty never clicks Connect and never sends a message. Every send is your click.
