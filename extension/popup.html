Runs in your own browser, in your logged-in MIT session. A daily alarm refreshes these pages automatically and flags what changed. Nothing leaves your machine unless you connect a MIghTy account below.
⚙️ Pages & settings
Watched pages
Follows the links on your current pages and adds the sub-pages on the same site (up to 40). Run it once per site; add other hubs (Canvas, registrar) manually, then discover again.
When on, the extension refreshes by itself daily (when Chrome is running). You can also hit “Refresh now” any time.
Optional · Claude summaries (Anthropic API key)
Without a key, you get “changed / unchanged” + a text preview (free). With a key, each change is summarized into a task. Stored only in this browser.
Optional · Sync to your MIghTy account (Supabase)
Pushes results to your MIghTy web app so they show on all your devices.
